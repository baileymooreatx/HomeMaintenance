# Recycle

For items not accepted by curbside recycling

## Batteries

**The City of Pflugerville Recycle Center** at is a local facility that accepts
batteries for recycling.

[City of Pflugerville Recycle Center](https://maps.app.goo.gl/U1W73SLMfJppjm7q8)
15500 Sun Light Near Way
Pflugerville, TX 78660

Single-use alkaline batteries can generally be disposed of in the regular trash
in Texas.

Additionally, you can drop off **rechargeable** AA and AAA batteries at
**Batteries Plus** stores or **The Home Depot** (via Call2Recycle bins) in
the area.

[Home Depot at 13309 N Interstate Hwy 35](https://maps.app.goo.gl/FQKH1GiC95EYoUH96)
Store #6553, phone **512-989-6650**
- ✅ **Rechargeable batteries** (AA, AAA, 9V, lithium-ion, NiMH, NiCd, etc.)  
  free drop-off in the **Call2Recycle bin** near the entrance. Must weigh under
  11 lbs and be under 300 watt-hours.
- ❌ **Single-use alkaline batteries** — not accepted. These can go in regular
  trash in Texas.

**Tip:** Call the store ahead at **512-989-6650** to confirm the recycling bin
is stocked and operational on the day you're going.

## Fluorescent Lightbulbs

Fluorescent bulbs contain mercury, so they **should not go in the trash**.  
Here are your best local options:  

- Home Depot – Pflugerville  
  [19101 Limestone Commercial Dr](https://maps.app.goo.gl/TWKesxSyh2KLrR8d8)  
  Accepts fluorescent tubes and CFLs for free drop-off.
  Call **512-990-5413** to confirm before going.
- Home Depot - Howard Lane    
  [13309 N Interstate Hwy 35](https://maps.app.goo.gl/FQKH1GiC95EYoUH96)  
  Store #6553, Phone **512-989-6650**  
    - ✅ **CFLs (compact fluorescent bulbs)** — free drop-off in the bin at the
      entrance. Place them in the plastic bags provided.
    - ⚠️ **Linear fluorescent tubes** (the long 4-ft/8-ft tubes) — Home Depot's
      program is specifically for **CFLs only**, not linear tubes. For those,
      your best options remain the **Austin/Travis County Recycle & Reuse Center
      ** (appointment required) or **Batteries Plus** (small per-tube fee).
      **Tip:** Call the store ahead at **512-989-6650** to confirm the recycling
      bin is stocked and operational on the day you're going.
- [Austin/Travis County Recycle and Reuse Center](https://maps.app.goo.gl/PjHk5mDrS82qtnf28)  
  Accepts fluorescent light bulbs as household hazardous waste. Free for Travis 
  County residents (which includes Pflugerville). Requires an **appointment** 
  via the [Austin Recycles](https://www.austintexas.gov/resource-recovery/locations/recycle-and-reuse-drop-center)  
  app or online.
- [Batteries Plus – Round Rock](https://maps.app.goo.gl/qXQ19amWG4b943J69) 
  Phone 512-600-7800 – Accepts fluorescent tubes for a small fee (~$2.49/tube).
- **Lowe's** – Some locations accept fluorescent bulbs, but policies vary by
  store. Call ahead to confirm.

**Tip:** Wrap unbroken tubes in their original packaging or tape them together
for transport. If a tube is already broken, keep everyone and pets out of the
room, air it out for 5–10 minutes, then carefully collect the debris per the
[EPA's broken bulb cleanup guide](https://www.epa.gov/mercury/recycling-and-disposal-cfls-and-other-bulbs-contain-mercury).

## Small Electronics

Here are your best options for small electronics (phones, tablets, chargers,
small appliances, etc.) in Pflugerville:

- **Best Buy** (Pflugerville location) – Free drop-off for small electronics
  like cell phones, tablets, laptops, and cables. Call 512-990-4699 to confirm
  current acceptance.
- **Travis County Recycle & Reuse Drop-off Center** – Accepts a wide range of
  e-waste for Travis County residents (Pflugerville is in Travis County). This
  is a reliable free option.
- **Z-Tech Global Solutions** – Near MoPac and 183; has a drop-off box at the
  back of their facility where you can leave electronics.
- **Office Depot** (19000 Limestone Commercial Dr) and **Verizon** (18801
  Limestone Commercial Dr) – Both accept **cell phones and streaming devices**
  for free drop-off.
- **Goodwill** – Accepts electronics even if non-functional and recycles them.

Note: The **City of Pflugerville Recycle Center** (15500 Sun Light Near Way) has
been reported by locals as **not** accepting electronics, so it's best to use
one of the options above instead.
